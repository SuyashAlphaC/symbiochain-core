## Accounting boundaries

- A closed trading cycle can produce an immutable performance-fee invoice: 15% of new cumulative profit after reported realized costs. Each payer's recorded losses must recover before more performance fees accrue. The high-water mark uses cumulative pre-performance-fee profit across that payer's crystallized cycles. Capital movements never enter that calculation. The accountant must assign every relevant customer cycle in settlement order; customer mandate enforcement and automatic cycle attribution are not implemented.
- An invoice is a receivable, not cash revenue. The customer agreement is represented by `agreedTermsDigest`; customer identity, consent, cycle attribution and completeness of submitted costs are operator/accountant attestations. This is not an audited multi-tenant custody or fee-collection system.
- Receipt confirmation uses a fixed HTTPS mainnet RPC, requires FINAL execution and successful receipts, matches an Intents `transfer` to `intents_executed`, and checks the NEP-245 asset movement in the same contract receipt. Exact payer, recipient, amount, token and post-invoice block time must match. One receipt/intent identity can be consumed once. A known related account cannot be the invoiced payer. This cannot detect undisclosed common ownership or prove a sale's commercial substance.
- Receipt verification trusts the mainnet RPC and the deployed `intents.near` contract; it is not a light-client or independent consensus proof. Invoice receipt recognition is covered by injected fixtures. A separate November 6 final wNEAR-credit parser check passed against a public third-party transaction; it is not a SymbioChain customer receipt or swap.
- Set `BUSINESS_INTENTS_ASSET` to the exact NEP-141 Intents token representing `BASE_ASSET` in `BASE_DECIMALS`, with no unit conversion. The operator must verify the token identity/decimals before configuration. Changing an existing book's denomination is refused. The receiver is frozen into invoice terms. Bridged tokens, native gas, USD micros and yoctoNEAR are distinct units.
- Business revenue is recorded separately from trading P&L, investor capital and internal agent payments. A cost window subtracts business operating costs from collected performance-fee revenue. Customer trading costs used to calculate the fee must not also be charged as a business expense unless the business actually bears them. Similarly, include only venue costs not already deducted as `settlement.feesAtoms` when crystallizing a cycle.
- Cost windows explicitly cover compute, gas, hosting, venue and other realized costs, including justified zero values. They require references, an accountable approver and reconciliation to all recorded inference charges. Unknown inference outcomes prevent closure. Quote allowances and receipt budgets are not realized expense evidence. Asset valuations are accountant-reported; no independent invoice/FX verification is claimed.
- Windows cannot overlap. Net values cover only their closed observation windows, not the entire lifetime or future operating costs. Unreconciled receipts are counted separately; full business runway and independently verified business profit remain unknown.
- A proceeds reservation requires positive net after recorded costs and recovery of recorded business losses. Serializable transactions prevent concurrent overspending. Outstanding inference outcomes anywhere in the book hold reservations until their charges are reconciled. A reservation begins **RESERVED_NOT_PAID**. Only the separate verified NFT funding state machine can consume an NFT reservation after actual settlement; compute reservations cannot be purchased. No payout can be confirmed by posting an arbitrary hash to this API.

## Server-only API

Reads require the existing read/admin bearer token. All writes require the admin bearer token. No public financial-write proxy is provided by the frontend. Financial records have no edit/delete endpoint. A reference cannot be reused with changed terms; an identical retry returns the prior entry.

| Endpoint                                         | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `GET /api/business/status`                       | Curated counts, asset atoms, closed-window net, cost scope, reservations and explicit unfinished capabilities. No payer identities, secrets or private source references.                                                                        |
| `POST /api/business/invoices`                    | Crystallize a closed cycle into a fee invoice. Fields: `reference`, `cycleId`, `payer`, `agreedTermsDigest`, `realizedCosts`, `inferenceCostUsdMicros`, `costSources`, `approvedBy`, `costsComplete: true`.                                      |
| `POST /api/business/invoices/:reference/receipt` | Read-only chain reconciliation using `txHash` and transaction `senderId`. Payer identity is taken from the executed intent, not the relayer. Available only in mainnet LIVE accounting.                                                          |
| `POST /api/business/windows`                     | Close a cost observation window with `reference`, UTC `from`/`through`, complete `realizedCosts`, `inferenceCostUsdMicros`, `costSources`, `approvedBy`, `costsComplete: true`. The interval is `[from, through)` using ledger recognition time. |
| `POST /api/business/reservations`                | Reserve `amountAtoms` from `windowReference` for `COMPUTE_PURCHASE` or `NFT_DISTRIBUTION`, under a stable `reference`. No transfer is submitted.                                                                                                 |

`realizedCosts` is an object containing integer-atom strings for **all** of `compute`, `gas`, `hosting`, `venue`, `other`. References are kept private. Do not put credentials or confidential customer content into source references. The released application already treats its read token as trusted infrastructure; it must remain server-only.

`BUSINESS_RECEIVER_ID`, `BUSINESS_INTENTS_ASSET`, and optional `BUSINESS_RELATED_ACCOUNTS` configure recognition only. They do not arm LIVE mode, create keys, fund accounts or bypass the trading/TEE gates. PAPER invoices and windows are fixtures, and PAPER cannot accept a mainnet receipt. The frontend `/business` consumes strict PAPER publication contracts through `/api/swarm/business` and `/api/swarm/funding`, rejects fabricated purchases/payments and retains an explicit stale state on failure. Its NFT code capability is implemented; execution stays disabled.

1. A funded, dedicated mainnet deployment with explicit gas/storage, trade and compute caps, exact token denomination and genuine customer mandate. A signed preview that loses money must continue to fail admission.
2. Actual profitable settlement and complete customer cost reconciliation, followed by an external fee payment. A customer-owned deposit, operator transfer or internal agent payment does not prove a fee-paying customer.
3. A provider-supported NEAR AI organization-bound purchase protocol. The reviewed public API exposes costs, balances and staking sync, not the required customer invoice/payment/credit-attribution contract. See [the provider review](PROVIDER_FUNDING.md). No fabricated endpoint, destination or privileged credit-limit mutation is used.
4. A durable proceeds-funded purchase adapter once that protocol exists: reserve earned cash and gas, persist exact payment identity before broadcast, hold unknown outcomes, reconcile the same invoice/payment on retry, verify final provider credit attribution to the Builders key's organization, then release the reservation. This adapter has not been implemented.
5. Funded validation of the implemented [NFT funding workflow](BUSINESS_FUNDING.md): use actual external proceeds, confirm conversion and native withdrawal, reconcile the treasury allocation, then demonstrate a holder claim. Code, sandbox payouts and the state-preserving testnet upgrade do not prove business revenue. No mainnet conversion or business-funded distribution has occurred.
